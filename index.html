How the numbers are built

Venues, units and station locations come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Residents, age mix, employment and country-of-birth shares come from the Eurostat Census 2021 1 km grid - the cell around each segment, always labelled as area context. Street-level crime is not shown: no comparable open feed exists across these countries. Urban rail flow and rents are MODELLED at city level from published system ridership and market reports - the rule is printed next to every figure. This is a planning tool for shortlisting, not a valuation.
