How this estimate is built

MODELLED rent uses the city's p25 / median / p75 annual rent per m² across scored segments. Ground-floor share applies a clearly shown 0.55-1.00 orientation factor. ESTIMATED build cost uses €400 / €650 / €950 per m², adjusted by premises condition. Equipment uses concept-specific orientation bands. Staffing converts your weekly opening pattern and people per shift into FTE at 40 hours/week, subtracting owner-covered hours. Registration, licences, deposits, fees, stock and contingency are explicit inputs or estimated country ranges. VAT, finance costs, business rates/local taxes, utilities and property-specific works are excluded because this static model cannot verify them. Nothing here is a quote.
