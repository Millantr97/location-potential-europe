Cost of opening calculator

Pick a city and a concept. The calculator combines the published rent model with estimated registration, licence, fit-out and staffing ranges. MODELLED and ESTIMATED figures are orientation for shortlisting, never quotes - verify with agents and authorities before budgeting.

Method: rent = the city's modelled annual rent per m2 (p25 / median / p75 across its scored segments) times your size. Registration and licences = estimated national ranges in EUR. Staffing = estimated monthly cost per full-time employee including employer contributions, in EUR. Fit-out = EUR 400-950 per m2 depending on standard. Nothing here is a quote.
