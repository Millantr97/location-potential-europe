The 10 best streets to open a laundrette or dry cleaner in Nuremberg (2026 data)

Laundrettes are a pure neighbourhood business: dense renters, few cars, weekly habits. The model scores exactly that - residents within walking distance and passing flow. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: A self-service laundrette with service washes: €7 average ticket, 12 machines, open seven days including evenings.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 309 Nuremberg street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Nuremberg urban rail ridership (annual, modelled at system level); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
