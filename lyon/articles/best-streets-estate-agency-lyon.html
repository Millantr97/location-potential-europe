Agencies win instructions where stock turns over and rivals prove the market works - but a corner too crowded kills the brand. The model balances affluent residents, visibility flow and existing agents. These ten streets scored highest.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 665 Lyon street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Lyon urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
