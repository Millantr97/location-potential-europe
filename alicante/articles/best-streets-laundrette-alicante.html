The 10 best streets to open a laundrette or dry cleaner in Alicante (2026 data)

Laundrettes are a pure neighbourhood business: dense renters, few cars, weekly habits. The model scores exactly that - residents within walking distance and passing flow. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: A self-service laundrette with service washes: £7 average ticket, 12 machines, open seven days including evenings.

Computed 17 September 2026 over 133 Alicante street segments. Every figure carries its label: OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 133 Alicante street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (No urban rail system - flow is MODELLED from commercial density only), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
