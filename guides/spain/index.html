How to open a business in Spain

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Spain. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Most small operators start as autonomo (sole trader) or an SL (limited company).

2. Register the business

Register with the Registro Mercantil for an SL, or directly with the tax agency (Agencia Tributaria) as autonomo. You need an NIE (foreigner ID number) first if you are not Spanish.

3. Tax registration

Get a NIF tax number and register for IVA (VAT) with the Agencia Tributaria (modelo 036/037).

4. Premises

Premises need a licencia de apertura (opening licence) from the ayuntamiento (town hall); works need a licencia de obras. Outdoor tables need a separate terrace licence.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service requires a sanitary registration and compliance with hygiene rules (APPCC/HACCP); alcohol is covered by the activity licence but hours are municipal.

6. Hiring

Employees are registered with the Seguridad Social before their first day.
