How to open a business in Ireland

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Ireland. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: sole trader or LTD (private company limited by shares).

2. Register the business

LTD: register with the Companies Registration Office (CRO). Sole trader: register with Revenue.

3. Tax registration

Register with Revenue for tax and VAT via ROS.

4. Premises

Check the premises' planning use with the local authority.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Register as a food business with the HSE / local authority environmental health; selling alcohol needs a licence via the courts/Revenue.

6. Hiring

Register as an employer with Revenue before the first payday.
