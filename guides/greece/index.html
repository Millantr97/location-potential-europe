How to open a business in Greece

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Greece. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: sole proprietorship or EPE/IKE (limited).

2. Register the business

Register with the General Commercial Registry (GEMI) via the one-stop shop; an IKE can be set up quickly.

3. Tax registration

Get an AFM tax number from AADE and register activity.

4. Premises

Opening a premises-based business needs a notification/licence via the municipality's system (e-adeies).

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs health-regulated operation approval; alcohol for on-site service follows the activity licence.

6. Hiring

Register employees with EFKA (social insurance) before starting.
