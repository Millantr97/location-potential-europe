How to open a business in The Netherlands

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in The Netherlands. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Typical forms: eenmanszaak (sole trader) or BV (limited company).

2. Register the business

Register with the KVK (Chamber of Commerce) - one visit registers the business.

3. Tax registration

The Belastingdienst issues the VAT number (BTW) automatically after KVK registration.

4. Premises

Premises use must match the municipal zoning plan (bestemmingsplan); check with the gemeente before signing.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service follows NVWA hygiene rules (HACCP); selling alcohol needs an alcohol licence from the gemeente under the Drank- en Horecawet.

6. Hiring

Register as an employer with the Belastingdienst; staff get standard contracts and minimum wage applies.
