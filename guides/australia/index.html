How to open a business in Australia

The five covered Australian cities use observed OpenStreetMap supply and ABS Census 2021 SA1 area context. Registration is national, while planning and many licences vary by state and council.

OBSERVEDAREA CONTEXTMODELLED

Use the location data first

Shortlist zones from observed premises and competition, area census context, and clearly labelled modelled flow, rent, fit and revenue. Then confirm premises availability, zoning, licences, taxes and staffing locally.

Practical sequence

  1. Choose a business structure and register it with the relevant authority.
  2. Confirm local zoning, building use, accessibility and signage rules for the exact premises.
  3. Check sector permits, including food, alcohol, health, fire and outdoor-trading rules where relevant.
  4. Price insurance, employment obligations, utilities and property-specific works before signing.

What varies most

Planning approval, food registration, liquor licensing, outdoor trading and employment details vary by state, territory and local council. Check the exact premises with the local council.

Official starting point

Start with the Australian Government start-a-business guide and ABLIS for licences and permits.
