How to open a business in Finland

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Finland. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: toiminimi (sole trader) or Oy (limited).

2. Register the business

Register with the Finnish Patent and Registration Office (PRH) Trade Register via the YTJ service.

3. Tax registration

Register for VAT (ALV) with the Tax Administration (Vero).

4. Premises

Check the premises' use with the municipality.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs notification to the municipal food control authority; alcohol service needs a licence from the Regional State Administrative Agency (AVI).

6. Hiring

Register as an employer with Vero; pension insurance (TyEL) is mandatory.
