How to open a business in United Kingdom

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in United Kingdom. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Sole trader or a private limited company (Ltd) are the usual choices.

2. Register the business

Ltd: register at Companies House. Sole trader: register with HMRC for Self Assessment.

3. Tax registration

Register with HMRC; VAT registration required above the threshold or voluntarily.

4. Premises

Check the premises' planning use class (Class E covers most shops/cafes); a change of use may need planning permission from the local council.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Register as a food business with the local council at least 28 days before opening. Selling alcohol needs a premises licence (and a personal licence holder).

6. Hiring

Register as an employer with HMRC (PAYE) before the first payday.
