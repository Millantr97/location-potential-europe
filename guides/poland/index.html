How to open a business in Poland

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Poland. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: sole proprietorship (CEIDG) or sp. z o.o. (limited).

2. Register the business

Register in CEIDG (sole trader) or the KRS court register (sp. z o.o.).

3. Tax registration

Get a NIP tax number and register for VAT with the tax office (urzad skarbowy) if applicable.

4. Premises

Check the premises' use with the gmina (municipality); a change of use may need approval.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs approval from the sanitary inspection (Sanepid); selling alcohol needs a municipal alcohol permit.

6. Hiring

Register employees with ZUS (social insurance) within 7 days.
