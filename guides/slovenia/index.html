How to open a business in Slovenia

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Slovenia. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: s.p. (sole trader) or d.o.o. (limited).

2. Register the business

Register via a SPOT point (e-VEM) - free for s.p.; d.o.o. via the AJPES register.

3. Tax registration

Get a tax number and register for VAT (DDV) with FURS above the threshold.

4. Premises

Check the premises' use with the municipality.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs notification and hygiene compliance with the health inspectorate.

6. Hiring

Register employees with ZZZS (health) and ZPIZ (pension).
