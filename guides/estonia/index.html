How to open a business in Estonia

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Estonia. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: FIE (sole trader) or OU (limited) - Estonia's e-Business Register is fully online.

2. Register the business

Register online in the e-Business Register (e-residents can do it from abroad).

3. Tax registration

Register for VAT (KMKR) with the Tax and Customs Board above the threshold.

4. Premises

Check the premises' use with the local government.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs notification to the Agriculture and Food Board (PTA).

6. Hiring

Register employees in the employment register before starting.
