How to open a business in Croatia

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Croatia. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: obrt (craft/sole trader) or d.o.o. (limited).

2. Register the business

Register via HITRO.HR or the court register (d.o.o.).

3. Tax registration

Get an OIB tax number and register for VAT (PDV) with the Tax Administration.

4. Premises

Check premises use with the local administration.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs sanitary inspection approval; hospitality trades are licensed activities.

6. Hiring

Register employees with HZMO (pension) and HZZO (health) before starting.
