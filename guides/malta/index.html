How to open a business in Malta

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Malta. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: sole trader or Ltd.

2. Register the business

Register with the Malta Business Registry (MBR).

3. Tax registration

Get a VAT number from the Commissioner for Revenue.

4. Premises

Premises need a development permit from the Planning Authority for the intended use class.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs a licence from the Malta Tourism Authority / health authorities; alcohol follows the trading licence.

6. Hiring

Register employees with Jobsplus before starting.
