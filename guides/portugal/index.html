How to open a business in Portugal

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Portugal. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: empresario em nome individual or Lda (limited company); Empresa na Hora can incorporate in one visit.

2. Register the business

Register via Empresa na Hora / Portal da Empresa; the company gets its NIPC number at incorporation.

3. Tax registration

Register activity with the Autoridade Tributaria (AT); IVA (VAT) registration at the same time.

4. Premises

Opening a shop or cafe needs a licenca de utilizacao from the Camara Municipal; works need prior notice or a licence.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food businesses register with ASAE / the municipality; terrace tables need a separate esplanada licence.

6. Hiring

Register with Seguranca Social as an employer and declare staff before they start.
