How to open a business in Slovakia

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Slovakia. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: zivnost (trade licence) or s.r.o. (limited).

2. Register the business

Register at the trade licensing office (okresny urad); an s.r.o. goes to the commercial register.

3. Tax registration

Register with the tax office (danovy urad); VAT above the threshold.

4. Premises

Check the premises' use with the building authority.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs approval from the regional public health authority (RUVZ).

6. Hiring

Register employees with the Social Insurance Agency and a health insurer.
