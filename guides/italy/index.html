How to open a business in Italy

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Italy. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: ditta individuale (sole trader) or SRL (limited company).

2. Register the business

Register with the Camera di Commercio (Registro delle Imprese), usually via the ComUnica single filing.

3. Tax registration

Get a partita IVA (VAT number) from the Agenzia delle Entrate.

4. Premises

Opening or changing a premises use usually needs a SCIA (certified start-of-activity notice) to the comune.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service requires hygiene compliance and, in many comuni, a professional requirement (SAB course or equivalent experience); alcohol for on-site consumption is covered by the SCIA but hours vary locally.

6. Hiring

Register employees with INPS and INAIL before starting.
