How to open a business in Belgium

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Belgium. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: sole trader (eenmanszaak/entreprise individuelle) or BV/SRL (limited).

2. Register the business

Register with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises (KBO/BCE) via an accredited enterprise counter.

3. Tax registration

Activate your VAT number with the Federal Public Service Finance.

4. Premises

Check the municipal urban-planning rules for the premises before signing.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service requires FASFC/AFSCA registration and hygiene compliance; alcohol service follows the activity registration.

6. Hiring

Register with a social secretariat and the ONSS/RSZ before hiring.
