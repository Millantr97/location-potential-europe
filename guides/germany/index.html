How to open a business in Germany

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in Germany. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Common forms: Einzelunternehmen (sole trader), UG or GmbH (limited).

2. Register the business

Register the trade at the local Gewerbeamt (trade office) - the Gewerbeanmeldung. A GmbH/UG also needs a notary and Handelsregister entry.

3. Tax registration

The Finanzamt issues your tax number after the trade registration; register for VAT (USt) in the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung via ELSTER.

4. Premises

Most retail needs no special premises licence; gastronomy serving alcohol needs a Gaststaettenerlaubnis from the local Ordnungsamt.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food businesses register with the Gesundheitsamt and staff need hygiene instruction (Infektionsschutzgesetz).

6. Hiring

Register employees for social insurance with the health insurer; minimum wage applies.
