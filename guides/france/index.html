How to open a business in France

The steps below are the typical path for opening a shop, cafe or restaurant in France. Rules change and municipalities differ - always verify with the authorities named at the end before committing money.

1. Choose a legal form

Typical forms: micro-entreprise (sole trader), EURL/SASU (single-owner companies), SARL/SAS.

2. Register the business

Register through the Guichet unique (procedures.gouv.fr) which routes to the greffe / INPI; you receive a SIRET number.

3. Tax registration

TVA (VAT) registration comes with the SIRET; choose your tax regime at registration.

4. Premises

Premises use is governed by the local PLU (zoning); a change of use (changement de destination) may need mairie approval.

5. Sector licences (food, alcohol)

Food service needs hygiene compliance (HACCP, DDPP inspection); selling alcohol to drink on-site requires a licence (petite licence or licence de debit de boissons) plus a permis d'exploitation training course.

6. Hiring

Declare hires to URSSAF before the first day (DPAE).
