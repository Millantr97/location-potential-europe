How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 1,064 Munich street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Munich urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
