The 10 best streets to open a pharmacy or chemist in Hamburg (2026 data)

Pharmacies trade on prescriptions and convenience: older and family residents, all-day flow, and distance from the multiples. These ten streets scored highest.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 714 Hamburg street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Hamburg urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
