The 10 best streets to open an estate or letting agency in Brussels (2026 data)

Agencies win instructions where stock turns over and rivals prove the market works - but a corner too crowded kills the brand. The model balances affluent residents, visibility flow and existing agents. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: An independent sales and lettings agency: €2,500 average fee equivalent, 8 negotiators, standard office hours plus Saturday.

Computed 13 September 2026 over 716 Brussels street segments. Every figure carries its label: OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

Read the labels, not just the score

OBSERVED measured at the street or station. AREA CONTEXT residents or borough statistics around it. MODELLED transparent estimates we compute, with the rule shown. For more accurate models consult a specialist.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 716 Brussels street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Brussels urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
