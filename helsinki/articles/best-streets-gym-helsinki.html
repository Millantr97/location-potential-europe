The 10 best streets to open a gym or fitness studio in Helsinki (2026 data)

Studios sell memberships to people who live and work nearby, so the model converts residents and commuter flows into members. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: A boutique fitness studio: €25 per class, 20 spots, peak classes before work and after work on weekdays.

Computed 13 September 2026 over 483 Helsinki street segments. Every figure carries its label: OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 483 Helsinki street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Helsinki urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
