Available premises: honest boundary

Location Potential does not claim that a scored street has a unit to rent or buy.

Why no listings layer is switched on: Europe has no comparable open, licensed, current feed of commercial availability. Major portals and agents use different coverage and terms; scraped links go stale and can misstate availability. OpenStreetMap commercial units are OBSERVED mapped places, not vacancies.

What is ready

  • The winning zone and coordinates can be shared or added to an expert brief.
  • The city map shows observed commercial units and same-category competitors.
  • A licensed partner feed can be connected later with source, checked date, asking price, tenure and listing URL shown per record.
