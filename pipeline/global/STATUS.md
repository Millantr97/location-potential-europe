# Admission status

## First build wave

- United States: 8 city configurations. Official ACS 2024 5-year tract tables and TIGER/Line 2024 tract boundaries confirmed available. Data files are fetched at build time and are not committed.
- Australia: Sydney is live with 340 scored segments, 6,383 observed commercial units and a 100.0% join to 10,436 ABS Census 2021 SA1 records. Melbourne is live with 373 scored segments, 8,326 observed units and a 100.0% join to 10,631 ABS SA1 records. Brisbane is live with 207 scored segments, 2,221 observed units and a 100.0% join to 5,260 ABS SA1 records. Perth and Adelaide remain configured for the next build wave.

## Held back

Canada, New Zealand and Japan are not in the first publication wave. Their national statistics sources are promising, but they do not ship until the country adapter produces at least 100 commercial street/area segments per city, 500 observed OSM objects, and a 90% official small-area-census join.
