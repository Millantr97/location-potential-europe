# Admission status

## First build wave

- United States: New York and Los Angeles are live. Los Angeles has 332 scored segments, 7,662 observed units and a 100.0% join to 2,498 ACS 2024 tracts. San Francisco is live with 198 segments, 5,996 observed units and a 100.0% join to 244 ACS tracts. Chicago is live with 466 segments, 8,952 observed units and a 100.0% join to 1,332 ACS tracts. Boston is live with 209 segments, 3,883 observed units and a 100.0% join to 746 ACS tracts. Washington, DC is live with 140 segments, 3,253 observed units and a 100.0% join to 206 ACS tracts. Seattle is live with 177 segments, 4,791 observed units and a 100.0% join to 495 ACS tracts. Miami was evaluated against official ACS 2024 tracts and current OSM, but is held back: the configured city bbox produced 91 scored zones, below the hard minimum of 100. Nothing was padded; it is not published.
- Australia: Sydney is live with 340 scored segments, 6,383 observed commercial units and a 100.0% join to 10,436 ABS Census 2021 SA1 records. Melbourne is live with 373 scored segments, 8,326 observed units and a 100.0% join to 10,631 ABS SA1 records. Brisbane is live with 207 scored segments, 2,221 observed units and a 100.0% join to 5,260 ABS SA1 records. Perth is live with 134 scored segments, 1,528 observed units and a 100.0% join to 4,281 ABS SA1 records. Adelaide is live with 139 scored segments, 1,197 observed units and a 100.0% join to 2,958 ABS SA1 records. The five-city Australian launch wave is complete.

## Held back

Canada, New Zealand and Japan are not in the first publication wave. Their national statistics sources are promising, but they do not ship until the country adapter produces at least 100 commercial street/area segments per city, 500 observed OSM objects, and a 90% official small-area-census join.
