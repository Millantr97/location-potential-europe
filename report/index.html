Market entry report, one city, one concept

The public tool answers "where could I open?" across 148 cities. The market entry report turns one of those answers into a document you can act on: one city, one concept, a five-zone shortlist with every number traced to its source and labelled OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

What is inside

SectionContents
1. Decision summaryThe recommended zone, the runner-up, and the one condition that would change the answer.
2. Five-zone shortlistFit score, modelled revenue band in local currency and EUR, rent band, day-rhythm profile, residents context - each figure labelled.
3. Competition mapNamed, recorded competitors of your concept inside each zone (OpenStreetMap), with chain/independent split.
4. Demand contextStation and street flow estimates, residents and demographics of the surrounding area, search-interest trend for the concept.
5. Risks and unknownsWhat the model cannot see: premises availability, licensing, seasonality, and the local checks to run before signing.
6. Verification planThe exact on-street counts, agent enquiries and authority checks to complete, with a one-page checklist.

What it is not

It is not a valuation, a lease negotiation, or proof that premises are available. Every estimate is a model output on public data, built to shortlist - the same honesty standard as the public tool. If the data for a city is thin, the report says so and shows where.
