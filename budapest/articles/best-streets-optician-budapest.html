The 10 best streets to open an optician or eyewear shop in Budapest (2026 data)

Opticians mix medical appointments with retail tickets: they need older, professional catchments and trust - and enough passing flow to fill the test room. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: An independent optician with retail frames: Ft180 average dispense, 1 test room, six days a week.

Computed 13 September 2026 over 1,101 Budapest street segments. Every figure carries its label: OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 1,101 Budapest street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Budapest urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
