The evidence chain

  1. Observed supply: mapped commercial units, named venues, stations, parks and services come from the published OpenStreetMap extract.
  2. Area context: residents and broad demographics use Census 2021 grid or UK census areas. They describe the surrounding area, not pavement footfall.
  3. Modelled decision support: station flows, typical rents, fit and revenue bands are transparent estimates. They are not valuations, profit forecasts or available-premises claims.

Scores rank the published candidates. They do not prove that a property is available, licensed, affordable or suitable. Verify the shortlist with an agent, an on-street count and a planning/licensing check.
