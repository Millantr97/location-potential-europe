Demand layers to investigate

Seasonality

Comparison records now flag a broad MODELLED seasonal-swing class. Before signing, compare monthly local tourism and transport releases. Do not treat an annual total as a flat month.

Walk catchment

Every city dataset exposes AREA CONTEXT residents for the surrounding official census geography and OBSERVED map anchors. A true 10-minute walk polygon needs a routing service and is not claimed by this static site.

Tourists vs locals

Residents are AREA CONTEXT. Visitors are not inferred from residents. Use official destination statistics and on-street counts to separate the two.

Weather resilience

The comparison feed carries a coarse MODELLED weather-sensitivity flag. Covered streets and indoor flow are not mapped consistently, so inspect the street and premises.

Commuter direction

Flow is MODELLED by day and concept trading window. Direction of movement is not available in the comparable public feeds; count both sides during opening hours.

Events

Recurring markets and festivals can move demand sharply. Check local council and destination calendars before relying on an event uplift.

Cost checklist

Staffing

Use official minimum-wage and employment-cost sources, then add employer tax, pension, leave and shift cover. The planner accepts your monthly quote rather than pretending one number fits 148 cities.

Utilities

Ask suppliers for a business tariff using premises size, equipment and opening hours. Domestic averages are not a defensible shop estimate.

Suppliers

Search wholesalers from the proposed address and test delivery days, minimum order and cold-chain needs. OpenStreetMap supplier density is too incomplete for a comparable score.

Rent per m²

The planner shows the published MODELLED rent benchmark per m² and stress-tests +10%. Replace it with the actual lease quote.

Housing nearby

Relocation is a separate decision. Compare official housing statistics and live rental listings near the shortlist; Location Potential does not mix household costs into the business score.

Startup estimate

Fit-out, deposit and fixed cost assumptions are editable in the business planner. Print the dossier and replace every assumption with a quote.
