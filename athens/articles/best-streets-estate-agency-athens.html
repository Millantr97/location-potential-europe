The 10 best streets to open an estate or letting agency in Athens (2026 data)

Agencies win instructions where stock turns over and rivals prove the market works - but a corner too crowded kills the brand. The model balances affluent residents, visibility flow and existing agents. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: An independent sales and lettings agency: €2,500 average fee equivalent, 8 negotiators, standard office hours plus Saturday.

Computed 13 September 2026 over 1,232 Athens street segments. Every figure carries its label: OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 1,232 Athens street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (Athens urban rail ridership (annual, published system figure); stop-level flow MODELLED), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 (1 km grid, Eurostat) residents for the local census grid cell (1 km), and rent context from city-level modelled rents. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
